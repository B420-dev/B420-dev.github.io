Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Asma Content Manager handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Access

Asma Content Manager may access information from your Pinterest account that is necessary to provide its content-management functionality.

This may include information related to your Pinterest account, Pins, and Boards, depending on the permissions you authorize.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the application's intended functionality, including organizing and publishing content to Pinterest when authorized by the user.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information accessed through the application is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide the requested functionality or when required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. Access to Pinterest data is limited to what is necessary for the application's functionality.

5. Pinterest

The application uses Pinterest's services and APIs. Your use of Pinterest remains subject to Pinterest's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Deletion

You may revoke the application's access to your Pinterest account through the available Pinterest account settings. You may also contact the application owner to request deletion of information associated with the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, please contact the application owner through the GitHub profile associated with this project.
